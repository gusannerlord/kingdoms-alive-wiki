# Alayen

Alayen is a companion with a story of his own: always the same young man, the same face, the same past. You meet him by doing a good turn to a village in Sturgia. He starts out green and afraid of a fight. His past comes out by the campfire a little at a time, and his quest, **"The Grey Wolf"**, ends at the den of the man who killed his father. Now and then he also asks for leave to see to things back home.

He comes once per campaign.

## How you meet him

Finish one of the game's own village quests **the generous way** in a Sturgian village. That means a village of Sturgian people, or one held by a Sturgian kingdom. Only these count:

| Village quest | The generous way |
|---|---|
| **{Village} Needs Grain Seeds** | Finish it at all: bring the grain yourself, or send a companion with the men and the money. The headman pays nothing for it. |
| **Village Needs Draught Animals** | When you deliver the animals and the headman begs to pay less, answer *"No problem, you can pay what you can afford."* |
| **Extortion by Deserters at {Village}** | After you drive the deserters off, take half the reward (*"Half of the coin is enough for our needs."*) or none (*"Keep your coin, headman. Your people's blessings are enough."*). |
| **Landowner Needs Manual Laborers** | Offer to share the profit with the villagers (*"[...] How about I share the profit with you and your people?"*), and then deliver the prisoners. |

Taking the full price, failing the quest, or any other village quest (driving a herd to a town, for example) does not count.

A few hours later, once you are out on the open map, a huge young man comes running after your party: *"Wait! Please, wait... Forgive me, I ran the whole way from {village}. You are the one who..."* He is Alayen, son of Radko the smith. He is eighteen, works the bellows at his dead father's forge, and the village calls him the Ox. He has never held a sword. He asks for no wage, only food and the chance to learn.

- *"Then ride with us, Alayen. We will make a fighter of you yet."* He joins for free, and your relation with him rises by **10**.
- *"What happened, the time your hands would not move?"* He will not tell you yet. He promises to tell you one night by the fire.
- *"I would take you, but my company cannot feed one more mouth now."* This is the only answer you have if you are at your clan's companion limit. He goes home to wait.
- *"A war band is no place for a farm boy. Go home, Alayen."* He goes home to wait, and he *"will not run after you twice"*.

If he goes home, a journal entry, **"The smith's son of {village}"**, marks his village on the map. Go there and pick **"Look for Alayen at the forge by the well"** in the village menu. You can then take him on (*"There is. Get your things, Alayen."*) or just say hello.

## Who he is at the start

Alayen starts at **level 1**, like a newly made character: very strong and tough, a little Athletics and Smithing, and almost no skill with weapons. He carries his father's peasant maul and a tunic. He is **merciful, generous and honest**, but **not brave** (his Valor is negative). Give him proper gear before you put him in the line.

At any time you can ask him *"How are you holding up, Alayen?"* to see how his story stands.

## His story by the fire

Alayen tells his story a chapter at a time, in a conversation that opens on the map while you travel. That never happens in a settlement, at sea, or during a battle. At least **2 days** pass between two of his talks, and each chapter waits for its moment:

| Chapter | When |
|---|---|
| The smith's son | About 2 days after he joins. |
| His first fight | After the first battle you win with him on the field. |
| His sister | After about two weeks with you. |
| A later chapter | Once his quest has moved on far enough. |
| What comes after | About 8 days after his quest ends. |

Each chapter ends with a choice of answers. All of them are kind enough, and they give **1 to 3** relation with him.

!!! tip "A red ribbon"
    When he talks about his sister Vesna, you can promise that the two of you will find her a red ribbon in the next big town. A journal entry, **"A red ribbon for Vesna"**, opens. Enter **any town** with Alayen in your party within **20 days**, and you choose the ribbon together in the market. Fail, and he buys a lesser one from a pedlar himself. He mentions how it went in a later chapter.

## His quest: The Grey Wolf

About **6 days** after he joins, and once he has told his first chapter, he finally tells you how his father died, as he promised on the road. Whatever you answer, the quest **"The Grey Wolf"** opens in your journal. There is no time limit: it moves at your pace.

1. **Teach him to fight.** The journal counts *"Fights won with Alayen at your side"*: you need **3**. A fight only counts if you win it and he is really on the field. Battles you autoresolve, fights he sits out wounded, and fights where you leave him out of the troops do not count. After each one you drill him (extra Two Handed and Athletics experience). He must also ride with you for about **three weeks** in all. If you finish the fights before that, the journal asks you to keep him with you a while longer.
2. **Word comes.** A couple of days later he brings news that leads to a **Sturgian town**, marked on your map. Go there with Alayen in your party.
3. **The den.** That leads on to a hideout, also marked on your map. Attack it with Alayen.

Take Alayen with you, unwounded, to the town and to the hideout: these are his fights. While the quest waits for you in a town or a hideout, he does not ask for errands.

??? note "Spoilers: The Grey Wolf, step by step"
    **The story.** Six winters ago, in a hungry year, a band of soldiers the boyars had stopped paying crossed the frozen river to Alayen's village for its seed grain. Their chief was Gedrik, called the Grey Wolf. Radko stood at the granary door with his hammer and said no. Alayen, twelve years old and already a head taller than his father, stood three steps behind him with an axe, and his hands would not move. The Wolf cut Radko down, laughed at the boy, and took the grain. Alayen's mother died the next winter.

    **The town.** A tinker brings word that **Skeggi Crowfoot**, the Wolf's man who held the torch that winter, drinks in a Sturgian town, usually the one nearest Alayen's village. If you can no longer enter that town (a war, a siege), Skeggi moves to another, and the journal says where. When you enter the town with Alayen, a window titled **"Skeggi Crowfoot"** opens:

    - *"Follow them into the alley"*: you and Alayen fight Skeggi and **3** of his friends in the town's back streets, as in the game's own alley fights. Nobody of yours dies there.
    - *"Not tonight"*: you can try again from the next day.

    | Outcome | Result |
    |---|---|
    | You win | Skeggi dies, and his purse holds **600 denars**. Before he dies he tells you where the Wolf dens. Alayen gains extra Two Handed experience. |
    | You lose | Skeggi gets away, but he does not leave town. Try again **3 days** later. |

    If Alayen is not with you, or one of you is wounded, a message tells you to come back.

    **The den.** The Wolf's camp is a hideout near Alayen's village, renamed **"Den of the Grey Wolf"** and marked on your map. It holds **26 to 34** of the Wolf's men, Sturgian foot soldiers gone bad, in two parties called **"The Grey Wolf's Band"** (more men the further your campaign has gone). **Gedrik Grey-Wolf** himself leads them and is the boss you face at the end, as in any hideout. He is a heavy old warrior in a warlord's helmet and lamellar armour, with a two-handed axe and throwing axes. If no hideout is free yet, or something else wipes out the band before you get there, the Wolf moves on, and word of his new den comes later.

    Alayen counts as being there only if he really fights in the hideout. If you go in by stealth, pick him among the men who follow at your signal. *"Send troops"* does not count.

    **The end.** After the den is cleared, Alayen has a last word with you on the map, and hands you **the Wolf's axe** (a Northlander Decorated Two Handed Axe).

    | | Alayen fought at the den | You went without him |
    |---|---|---|
    | Relation with Alayen | **+10** | **+3** (**+5** if you answer *"I am sorry, Alayen. I should have waited for you."*) |
    | His character | He becomes **valorous**. Answer *"Your father would be proud of you, Alayen."* and he also becomes **honourable**. | He is no longer cowardly. |
    | For you | **4 renown**, and **+5** relation with the notables of his village | the same |

    About 8 days later, his last chapter by the fire tells you how the ribbon went and what he means to do next.

## His errands

Like the companions in [Companion errands](companion-errands.md), Alayen sometimes asks for leave and a few men. The first request comes about **12 days** after he joins, and then one every **22 to 40 days**. He uses the same conversation and the same troop screen, but asks for a handful of men for **5 to 12** days. He never asks for more than about a third of your seasoned soldiers.

| Errand | When he may ask | What comes of it |
|---|---|---|
| **Seed grain for {village}**: buying cheap seed in the nearby market town and guarding the cart home | Any time, unless his village has been raided | His village grows a little, and its notables like you a bit better. Steward experience. |
| **Brigands above {village}**: driving bandits away from his mother's people, the charcoal burners | After his first battle, or 20 days with you | The town his village belongs to becomes a bit safer. Two Handed experience. The only risky one: some of the men usually fall, and about 1 time in 5 it goes badly, with more losses and Alayen back wounded. |
| **Vesna's name day**: walking his sister round the village with drums and ribbons | Once, after his third chapter | A little relation with his village. Charm experience. |
| **Radko's forge**: putting the roof back on his father's forge and lighting it once more | Once, after his quest ends or 45 days with you | He brings you **five bars of good iron** he worked himself. Lots of Smithing experience. |

Letting him go gives **+4 to +6** relation with him, and refusing costs **3 to 5**. While he is away, the errand has its own journal entry.

## If he leaves or dies

If Alayen dies, or leaves your clan, his quest is cancelled.

## Turn it off

In the settings file, set the switch to `false`. See [Settings](../settings.md).

- `StoryCompanionsEnabled`: no companion with a story comes into your campaign. If Alayen is already with you, he stays as an ordinary companion and his story stands still.
- `AlayenEnabled`: the same, for Alayen alone.
- `AlayenQuestEnabled`: no quest "The Grey Wolf". He still tells his story by the fire, in a version where he never goes after the Wolf. A quest already under way rests until you turn it back on.
- `AlayenErrandsEnabled`: he never asks for leave and men.
