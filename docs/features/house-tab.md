# Your house at a glance

Kingdoms Alive gives your own house two places of its own: the **House** tab of the clan screen, for what your house believes, claims and remembers, and your house's panel under **Fealty** in the [Realm tab](royal-court.md#the-realm-tab) of the kingdom screen, for where it stands in the realm: your lord, your titles, your knights and your dues. Hover over any line to read what it means; a line that names a town, a castle, a house or a lord opens its page in the encyclopedia.

Every house, yours included, still shows its faith, its rights and its claim to the throne in the encyclopedia.

## What it shows {#what-it-shows}

The **House** tab is the fifth tab of the **clan screen** (L), next to Members, Parties, Fiefs and Income, laid out like the game's Fiefs tab. It is always there, whether your house serves a realm, fights for pay or answers to no one. Each section appears only while the system behind it is on.

- **Faith**: the faith your house keeps and how its priests see you, with a bar toward your next standing (unknown to the priests, known at the shrines, one of the faithful, a pillar of the faith), like the renown bar at the top of the screen. Under it, what the faith asks of its faithful in a priest's words, the crown's faith, the blessing on your company if you carry one, and whether the festival or the sacred months are under way. See [The faiths of Calradia](faiths.md).
- **Claims**: every town and castle your house has a right to, firm or weak, and where the right comes from; your claims to titles; and the rights other houses hold to your own lands and titles. Here you only read them: to press a right, go to **Claims** in the Realm tab, and the tooltip of a right says so when that road is open to you. See [Rights to fiefs](fief-rights.md).
- **Grievances**: the wrongs other houses hold against you, and those your house remembers, each marked fresh, fading or old. The same lines stand under **Your standing** in the Realm tab.

The tab shows no numbers behind the rules: the bars show how far you have come, not by how much.

## Your house in the Realm tab {#your-house-in-the-realm-tab}

Under **Fealty** in the Realm tab, your own house has its panel on the right, and its card in the tree stands out from the rest. What is yours (your house, your vassals and knights, the entries that are yours or aim at your house in every section) bears the gold frame of the game and comes first, so you can tell at a glance what is your own and what belongs to the other houses of the realm.

Your house's panel shows:

- **Your lord**: by name and house, with the seat you hold of him by and why (*"{Seat}, your first seat, lies in the county of {County}, which the {Lord} hold: you hold your land of them."*), or that you hold of the crown itself, or that you rule and answer to no one. See [Liege and Vassals](titles.md#liege-and-vassals).
- **Your titles**: duke, count, castellan, or their names in your realm; a title that sleeps because none of its towns is held in your realm now.
- **Your vassals**: the houses and knights that hold land of yours. The vassal of your vassal is not yours.
- **Your knights**: the landed knights sworn to you and their villages, how many **knights your house may have** (one to each castle), which of your castles still have none, and the **"Grant land to a worthy knight"** button (in the empires, for the throne, **"Grant a pronoia to a worthy knight"**), greyed with the reason when you cannot. If you are a landed knight yourself, your oath and the lord you hold your village of. See [Landed knights](landed-knights.md).
- **Your dues**: what your house pays its lord today and what each of your vassals pays you, in denars.

On the left of the Realm tab, **Your standing** gathers the rest: your relation with your ruler; whether your house has a claim to the throne of your realm, and why (with one you may lead a plot as its pretender; without one, its tooltip tells you how a house of your realm comes by a claim, where your house stands by its right to rule and where it falls furthest short: see [How a house wins a claim](succession.md#how-a-house-wins-a-claim)); whether your house is marked as traitor after a failed rising and waits for the crown's judgment; the offices your house holds at court; your oath of fealty; the plot, cause or feud your house is part of; your allies, with what binds you to each; **Your lands**, the towns and castles your house holds against how many the lords of the realm bear with in its hands, and whether they begrudge it; and the grievances. See [Traitors and the houses of a realm](realm-houses.md), [The royal court](royal-court.md) and [Factions and grievances](factions.md).

With the tree of the realm switched off in the settings, Fealty shows your own house alone, with all of the above.
