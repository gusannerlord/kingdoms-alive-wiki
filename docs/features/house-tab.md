# Your house at a glance

Kingdoms Alive adds a fifth tab to the **clan screen** (L), next to Members, Parties, Fiefs and Income: **House**. It gathers in one place what the mod tracks about your own house, laid out like the game's Fiefs tab. Hover over any line to read what it means; a line that names a town, a castle, a house or a lord opens its page in the encyclopedia.

The tab is only for your house. Every house, yours included, still shows its faith, its rights and its claim to the throne in the encyclopedia.

## What it shows {#what-it-shows}

Each section appears only while the system behind it is on.

- **Faith**: the faith your house keeps and how its priests see you, with a bar toward your next standing (unknown to the priests, known at the shrines, one of the faithful, a pillar of the faith), like the renown bar at the top of the screen. Under it, what the faith asks of its faithful in a priest's words, the crown's faith, the blessing on your company if you carry one, and whether the festival or the sacred months are under way. See [The faiths of Calradia](faiths.md).
- **Claims**: every town and castle your house has a right to, firm or weak, and where the right comes from; and the rights other houses hold to your own lands. See [Rights to fiefs](fief-rights.md).
- **In the realm**: whether your house has a claim to the throne of your realm, and why (with one you may lead a plot as its pretender; who wears the crown, and how firmly, is in the Crown tab of the kingdom screen: see [The crown and its claim](succession.md)); without one, its tooltip tells you how a house of your realm comes by a claim, where your house stands by its right to rule and where it falls furthest short (see [How a house wins a claim](succession.md#how-a-house-wins-a-claim)); whether your house is marked as traitor after a failed rising and waits for the crown's judgment (a pardon, a rebuke before the court, the loss of land or banishment; you may bow to it, or leave the realm with nothing when land is taken); the offices your house holds at court and what they do; your oath of fealty; the plot, faction or feud your house is part of; and your allies, with what binds you to each. Under **Your lands**, the towns and castles your house holds against how many the lords of the realm bear with in its hands, and whether they begrudge it (hover over it for when they would and why). See [Traitors and the houses of a realm](realm-houses.md), [The royal court](royal-court.md) and [Factions and grievances](factions.md).
- **Liege and Vassals**: your house's titles (duke, count, castellan, or their names in your realm), a title that sleeps because none of its towns is held in your realm, and your claims to titles, firm or weak; **your liege**, by name and house, with the seat you hold of him by (or the crown), and his own lord above him up to the crown, or that you rule and answer to no one; **your vassals**, the houses and knights that hold land of yours, each with its own vassals one level below, the mightiest marked with a red blade; and **today's dues**, paid to your lord and received from each vassal, in denars. Then the landed knights: those sworn to you and their villages, how many **knights your house may have** (one to each castle), and the **"Grant land to a worthy knight"** button (in the empires, for the throne, **"Grant a pronoia to a worthy knight"**), greyed with the reason when you cannot; or, if you are a landed knight yourself, your oath and the lord you hold your village of. See [Liege and Vassals](titles.md#liege-and-vassals) and [Landed knights](landed-knights.md).
- **Grievances**: the wrongs other houses hold against you, and those your house remembers, each marked fresh, fading or old.

The tab shows few numbers behind the rules: the bars show how far you have come, not by how much. Your lands is the exception: it counts your towns and castles against how many the lords bear with.
