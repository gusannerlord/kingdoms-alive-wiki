# Mercenary contracts and the Unsworn

The ruler who pays you now puts you to work: a messenger stops you on the road with a task, and a good record opens the way to vassalage, and maybe a castle. Some tasks pit you against the **Unsworn**, a new company of sellswords who answer to no crown and who you can also hire.

## How tasks arrive

- Only while your house serves a kingdom **as mercenaries**.
- A messenger of the ruler finds you on the map. The first comes about **5 days** after you sign. After that, one every **15 to 25 days**, counted from the last messenger, and never sooner than **5 days** after your last task ended.
- **One task at a time.** No messenger comes while a task is under way.
- The messenger waits until you are out on the map and free: not while you are a prisoner, at sea, in a town, castle or village, in a battle, a siege or a menu. If they cannot reach you for **5 days**, they give up, with no penalty. The days you spend riding in someone's army do not count towards those 5.
- No messengers come while the ruler is a prisoner. If there is nothing to ask of you nearby, the ruler looks again **3 days** later.

## The messenger's offer

The messenger describes the task, its deadline and reward. Answer **"Tell her/him it will be done."** (it goes into your quest journal) or **"Tell her/him I cannot take this on."** (a refusal). Close the talk without answering and they come back 12 hours later.

## "In the service of the crown"

The first messenger of each service also tells you the deal and opens a quest in your journal: **"In the service of the crown of {KINGDOM}"**. It has two bars:

- **Contracts fulfilled**: **0 of 5**.
- **House standing**: your clan must be **tier 2** or higher.

Each task given, fulfilled, failed or refused gets a line in it. With **5 tasks fulfilled** and your clan at tier 2, the quest is complete and the ruler makes you **the game's own offer of vassalage** (or tells you to go and ask): go and speak with them in person to swear. If you swear to that ruler some other way first, the quest ends well, without the castle; if you leave the service or the contract is ended, it fails.

If the ruler's own clan holds **more than 2 castles**, they also promise you one: swear within **60 days** and they give you the least prosperous castle of their clan (never a town). Otherwise the oath brings no fief.

## The tasks

| Task | Deadline | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| Raid an enemy territory | 60 days | The game's own: 15,000 denars, 5 renown, 20 influence, honor, +10 with the ruler |
| Escort a caravan | 30 days | 2,000 denars, 3 renown, +5 with the ruler |
| Guard a border village | 30 days | 3,000 denars, 3 renown, +5 with the ruler |
| Clear an Unsworn hideout | 30 days | 4,000 denars, 3 renown, +5 with the ruler |
| Sweep of the Unsworn bands | 50 days | 2,000 denars, 3 renown, +5 with the ruler |
| Rescue a lord | 50 days | 8,000 denars (12,000 for one of the ruler's clan), 5 renown, +3 with the ruler, +5 with the lord |

### Raid an enemy territory
The game's own **"Raid an Enemy Territory"** quest, given by messenger: burn **3 villages** of the enemy kingdom whose villages lie nearest to you. The game pays its usual reward the moment the third village burns. A village burnt in a fight as time runs out still counts. Only offered while the ruler has no other quest of their own open.

### Escort a caravan
One of the realm's caravans near you. Ride with it and **be close by when it enters a town**, within 30 days. A town it reaches in the first 12 hours, or without you near, does not count: see it into the next one. If an enemy destroys it, the task fails.

### Guard a border village
A village of the realm close to an enemy town or castle. Keep it **in sight for 3 days in all** within 30 days, like the game's quest to scout enemy garrisons: every hour it is within your party's sight counts. A message marks each day (*"Another day of watch over {VILLAGE} is done (1/3)."*). If the village is looted first, it fails. Beating an enemy lord's party at or near the village fulfils it at once.

### Clear an Unsworn hideout
A hideout near the realm's villages, taken over by the Unsworn and marked on your map as **"Unsworn Hideout"**. Inside are **26 to 32** Unsworn on foot, more as your campaign advances, and no common bandits. Attack it as any hideout, or use "Send troops". The last fight is against the **Unsworn Captain**, the hideout's own chief, with the game's usual duel offer.

### Sweep of the Unsworn bands
**Three bands** of Unsworn, each of **20 to 40** men (more as your campaign advances) and led by a veteran, roam around villages of the realm away from you. Each has an arrow on the edge of the map pointing to it. Beat all three within 50 days. They will not talk or surrender, and they come after you if you are near and they are clearly stronger (more than 1.2 times your strength). The lords of the map leave them alone, so it is up to you.

### Rescue a lord
A lord of the realm held in the **dungeon** of an enemy town or castle within reach (never one carried by a party). One of the ruler's own clan is picked first, then the nearest. Free them in any of three ways:

- the game's own **prison break** in that town or castle;
- **taking the place** in a battle you or your army fight;
- if the enemy moves them meanwhile, **beating the party that carries them**.

Where they are held is marked on the map. While the task is under way, the lord almost never escapes on their own (about 1 in 8 over the 50 days, instead of nearly always in the base game). If they go free some other way (ransom, peace, someone else) or die, the task lapses with no penalty.

## War and peace

- **In war**, a lord to rescue comes first: when there is one, the rescue weighs 4 times as much as any other task in the draw (8 times for one of the ruler's clan). Raids and the protection task (a caravan escort or a border village, one or the other) weigh 1 each. The two Unsworn tasks are rare at war (about a quarter of that).
- **In peace**, there are no raids, border villages or rescues: you get the caravan escort and the two Unsworn tasks.

## Failing and refusing

A refusal or a failed task (time ran out, the caravan lost, the village looted):

- your **mercenary pay is cut by half for 20 days**;
- **-2 relation** with the ruler, but never more than **-5** in all with the same ruler;
- **three in a row** and the ruler ends your contract: *"{KINGDOM} no longer wants your sword: your contract as a mercenary has been ended."* You are warned after the second. Any fulfilled task resets the count.

A task **lapses**, with no penalty, through no fault of yours: you were a prisoner when time ran out, the ruler is no longer on the throne, the caravan leaves the realm's roads for another reason, or the village changes hands.

## The Unsworn troop tree

Sellswords of no culture. Their names in the party screen:

| Tier | Troop | Upgrades to |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Unsworn Recruit | Footman, or Outrider (needs a horse) |
| 2 | Unsworn Footman | Wilder or Trained Bowman |
| 2 | Unsworn Outrider | Raider or Horse Archer |
| 3 | Unsworn Wilder | Warmonger or Hellion |
| 3 | Unsworn Trained Bowman | Longbowman |
| 3 | Unsworn Raider | Battle Rider (needs a war horse) |
| 3 | Unsworn Horse Archer | Mounted Master Archer (needs a war horse) |
| 5 | Unsworn Warmonger, Hellion, Longbowman, Battle Rider, Mounted Master Archer | — |

Where to get them:

- **Taverns.** When a town's tavern changes the mercenaries it offers (every two days, as in the base game), about **1 time in 5** they are Unsworn instead, mostly of the lower tiers. You hire them from the tavern's mercenary like any other.
- AI lords and caravans hire them from the same taverns, so you will also meet Unsworn in other parties.
- **Prisoners.** Like any regular troop of **tier 2 or higher**: every Unsworn except the Recruit.

## Turn it off
In the settings file, set the switch to `false`. See [Settings](../settings.md).

- `MercContractsEnabled`: no more tasks by messenger, and no "In the service of the crown" quest. A task under way still ends as usual.
- `MercContractRaid`: no raid tasks.
- `MercContractProtect`: no caravan escorts or border villages to guard.
- `MercContractUnswornHideout`: no Unsworn hideouts to clear.
- `MercContractUnswornSweep`: no sweeps of Unsworn bands.
- `MercContractRescue`: no rescues.
- `MercContractVassalPath`: no "In the service of the crown" quest, no vassalage offer or castle; the tasks go on.
- `UnswornTroopsEnabled`: taverns no longer offer the Unsworn, and the two Unsworn tasks are not given.
