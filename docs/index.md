# Diplomacy - Kingdoms Alive

A campaign mod for Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord. Kingdoms go to war for a reason, wars last long enough to matter, peace is agreed by both sides, and peace is no longer empty: lords feast and duel, clans quarrel and make up, great realms split in civil war, and your own household gives you something to do.

Every feature can be switched off on its own (see [Settings](settings.md)).

!!! warning "This mod is in beta"
    I am still testing things. Right now events may happen quite often, and you may see several within a few days. **That is on purpose**: it lets every feature be tested and bugs found quickly. Frequencies and values will be tuned over the next updates with your feedback.

    I will be very active these days. If you find a problem, report it (see [Bugs](faq.md#reporting-a-bug)) and I will fix it quickly.

## What the mod does

### War and diplomacy
- **[War and peace](features/war-and-peace.md)**: wars have a cause you can read, last weeks instead of days, and end when both sides agree.
- **[Events between kingdoms](features/events-between-kingdoms.md)**: raids, insults and gifts between neighbours build tension that can lead to war, or prevent it.
- **[Winning the council over](features/council.md)**: talk lords into backing your war or your peace.

### Life at court
- **[Feasts and the grand tournament](features/feasts.md)**: lords gather in peacetime; you can attend or hold your own, and a ruler can add a grand tournament.
- **[Events between clans](features/clan-events.md)**: the clans of a kingdom fall out and make up.
- **[Civil war](features/civil-war.md)**: large kingdoms crack, powerful clans plot for the throne, and you can join, oppose or lead the plot.
- **[Envoys](features/envoys.md)**: send a family member or companion to a lord with a message.
- **[Duels](features/duels.md)**: a lord who hates you may challenge you to a duel to the death.

### Your realm and household
- **[Affairs of your kingdom](features/realm-affairs.md)**: sellswords, revolts, famines, feuds and great works to deal with in peacetime.
- **[Companion errands](features/companion-errands.md)**: companions ask for leave to settle matters of their own.

### Campaign
- **[Fast mode families](features/fast-mode.md)**: in fast mode, noble houses no longer die out.
- **[Other changes](features/other-changes.md)**: longer sieges, less peacetime training for AI troops, fairer gear prices.
