# FAQ, bugs and roadmap

## Reporting a bug
I fix reported problems quickly. Report them in the **Bugs** or **Posts** tab on [Nexus Mods](https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/13980).

A good report says:

1. **What happened** and what you expected instead.
2. **When**: what you were doing (on the map, in a town, in a conversation...).
3. **Your mod list**, or at least the mods that touch the campaign.
4. If the game crashed: accept the crash report, and tell me the date and time of the crash.

!!! tip "Feedback is just as welcome"
    I really value your feedback to keep tuning the numbers. If something happens too often, too rarely, or feels unfair, tell me.

## Frequent questions

**Events happen too often, or too rarely, for my taste. Can I change that?**
Yes. Every feature has its own switch, and its numbers can be changed in the settings file (see [Settings](settings.md)). And tell me: frequencies are tuned with players' feedback.

**Can I add the mod to my current campaign?**
You can, but it is best to start a new campaign with it. See [Getting started](getting-started.md#new-or-existing-campaign).

**Can I remove it later?**
Not safely. Removing it halfway through a campaign can break that save. See [Removing the mod](getting-started.md#removing-the-mod).

**Where do I see a companion or family member I sent away?**
In your quest log. Everyone you send away on an [affair of your kingdom](features/realm-affairs.md) or a [companion errand](features/companion-errands.md), and a house of your realm you hand an affair to, has an entry there with a **Return Days** bar. It closes by itself when they are back. [Envoys](features/envoys.md) are not in the log: they write to you instead (messages starting *"A letter from..."*).

**Where do I follow my rival or my mercenary tasks?**
Also in your quest log. A rivalry has its own entry, *"Rivalry: ..."* (see [Your rival](features/rival.md)). As a mercenary, your service shows as *"In the service of the crown of ..."*, and each task the ruler sends you has an entry of its own (see [Mercenary contracts](features/mercenary-contracts.md)).

**Where are the mod's new tabs?**
In the kingdom screen (**K**), after Diplomacy: **Court** ([The royal court](features/royal-court.md)), **Plots** ([Civil war](features/civil-war.md)) and **Vassals** ([Vassal realms](features/vassal-realms.md)).

**Does it work with the Diplomacy mod?**
No. Both change how kingdoms decide on war and peace. Use one or the other.

**I don't like one feature. Can I keep the rest?**
Yes. Every feature has its own switch. See [Settings](settings.md).

**Does it work in normal (non-fast) campaigns?**
Yes. Only the extra AI marriages and children on the [fast mode families](features/fast-mode.md) page are limited to fast mode.

**Does it work with War Sails?**
Yes.

**Is it in English only?**
For now, yes.
