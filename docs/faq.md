# FAQ, bugs and roadmap

## Reporting a bug
I will be very active these days and fix reported problems quickly. Report them in the **Bugs** or **Posts** tab on [Nexus Mods](https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/13980).

A good report says:

1. **What happened** and what you expected instead.
2. **When**: what you were doing (on the map, in a town, in a conversation...).
3. **Your mod list**, or at least the mods that touch the campaign.
4. If the game crashed: accept the crash report, and tell me the date and time of the crash.

!!! tip "Feedback is just as welcome"
    I really value your feedback to finish tuning the numbers. If something happens too often, too rarely, or feels unfair, tell me. That is exactly what the beta is for.

## Frequent questions

**Events happen very often. Is that normal?**
Yes, for now. In the beta the events are frequent on purpose, so every feature gets tested and bugs show up quickly. They will be spaced out as feedback comes in.

**Can I add the mod to my current campaign?**
Yes. See [Getting started](getting-started.md#new-or-existing-campaign).

**Can I remove it later?**
Yes, the save still loads. See [Removing the mod](getting-started.md#removing-the-mod).

**Does it work with the Diplomacy mod?**
No. Both change how kingdoms decide on war and peace. Use one or the other.

**I don't like one feature. Can I keep the rest?**
Yes. Every feature has its own switch. See [Settings](settings.md).

**Does it work in normal (non-fast) campaigns?**
Yes. Only the extra AI marriages and children on the [fast mode families](features/fast-mode.md) page are limited to fast mode.

**Does it work with War Sails?**
Yes.

**Is it in English only?**
For now, yes.
